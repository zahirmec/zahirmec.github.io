01 / WHO WE ARE

ABOUT MECSIS

Practical engineering.
Dependable execution.

MECSIS Engineering Ltd. is a Bangladesh-based engineering consultancy and contracting company established in 2012, with Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) engineering as its primary business focus.

We support industrial, commercial, residential, infrastructure development and power-sector projects through practical engineering solutions, coordinated site execution and a strong focus on workmanship, safety and client service.

Our capabilities also include steel building fabrication and erection, factory sheds and structural works as complementary specializations.
